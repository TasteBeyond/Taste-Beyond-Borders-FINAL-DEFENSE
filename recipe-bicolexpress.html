Bicol Express

Philippines

Bicol Express Explanation

Bicol Express is a popular Filipino dish known for its rich, creamy, and spicy flavor. It is made with pork cooked in coconut milk, chili peppers, shrimp paste, garlic, onion, and other seasonings. The dish is strongly associated with the Bicol Region, which is known for its love of spicy food and the use of coconut milk in many traditional dishes. Its combination of creamy coconut milk and spicy chili gives Bicol Express its distinct and flavorful taste.


Did You Know About Bicol Express?

Did you know that Bicol Express is named after the passenger train that travels between Manila and the Bicol Region? The dish became popular because of its rich coconut-based sauce and spicy flavor, which reflects the food culture commonly associated with Bicol.

Ingredients

  • Pork Belly or Pork Shoulder
  • Coconut Milk
  • Coconut Cream
  • Chili Peppers
  • Shrimp Paste (Bagoong Alamang)
  • Garlic
  • Onion
  • Ginger
  • Cooking Oil
  • Salt
  • Ground Black Pepper

How to Prepare

  1. Heat the cooking oil in a pan over medium heat.
  2. Sauté the garlic, onion, and ginger until fragrant and softened.
  3. Add the pork and cook until it becomes lightly browned.
  4. Add the shrimp paste and mix well with the pork.
  5. Pour in the coconut milk and stir to combine.
  6. Cover the pan and simmer until the pork becomes tender.
  7. Add the chili peppers and coconut cream.
  8. Continue simmering until the sauce becomes thick and creamy.
  9. Season with salt and ground black pepper according to taste.
  10. Stir well, remove from heat, and serve the Bicol Express while hot.
